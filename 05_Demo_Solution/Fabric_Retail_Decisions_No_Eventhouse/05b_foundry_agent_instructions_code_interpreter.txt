RETAIL INVESTIGATION SEQUENCE
When investigating the electronics decline:
1. Quantify September 2026 vs September 2025 electronics revenue (totals and % change).
2. Identify which stores contribute most to the decline (rank by absolute decline).
3. Test the inventory hypothesis - the snapshot is a single dated observation and
   cannot prove month-long shortages.
4. Examine promotion and store-operations evidence.
5. Incorporate new_evidence only when explicitly asked.
6. Separate verified observations, plausible hypotheses, and unknowns. Correlation
   is not proof of root cause.
